# Acme Corp Travel & Expense Policy

Owner: Finance · Version 3.0 · Applies to all employees and contractors travelling on Acme business

## 1. Booking travel

- All flights, trains and hotels must be booked through the corporate travel portal **TravelDesk**.
- **Flights under 6 hours:** economy class.
- **Flights of 6 hours or more:** premium economy; business class requires written approval from a Vice President.
- Rail is preferred over flights for journeys under 4 hours.
- Book at least **14 days in advance** where possible; late bookings must include a justification.

## 2. Accommodation

- Hotel cap: **EUR 180 per night** in most cities.
- Higher-cost cities (London, New York, San Francisco, Zurich, Tokyo): **EUR 250 per night**.
- Airbnb or similar rentals are allowed if cheaper than the hotel cap and booked via TravelDesk.

## 3. Meals and per diem

- Daily per diem for meals: **EUR 60 within Europe**, **EUR 75 outside Europe**.
- Client entertainment above **EUR 100 per person** requires pre-approval from your manager.
- Alcohol is reimbursable only as part of approved client entertainment.

## 4. Submitting expense claims

- Submit expense claims in **Expensify within 30 days** of the expense.
- Itemised receipts are required for every expense above **EUR 25**.
- Claims are approved by your line manager; claims above **EUR 2,000** also require Finance approval.
- Reimbursement is paid with the next monthly payroll after approval.

## 5. Not reimbursable

- Personal travel insurance (Acme provides worldwide business travel cover).
- Fines (parking, speeding), minibar charges, upgrades paid for personal convenience.
- Lost-luggage costs that are covered by the airline.
