# Acme Corp Remote & Hybrid Work Policy

Owner: People Team (HR) · Version 2.1

## 1. Hybrid working model

- Employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week**, agreed with their manager.
- Teams agree on at least **one shared anchor day** per week in the office.
- Some roles (reception, lab, facilities) are office-based and excluded from this policy.

## 2. Working from abroad

- Employees may work from another country for up to **20 working days per calendar year**.
- Requests must be submitted in Workday at least **15 working days before** travel and approved by the manager and the People Team (tax and immigration check).
- Working from sanctioned countries is never permitted.

## 3. Home office equipment and allowances

- One-time **home office stipend of EUR 500** for a desk, chair or monitor, claimable in the first 6 months of employment.
- Monthly **internet allowance of EUR 30**, paid via payroll.
- Acme laptops and monitors remain company property and must be returned when leaving Acme.

## 4. Security when working remotely

- Always connect through the Acme **VPN** when using public or shared Wi-Fi.
- Do not let family members or friends use your Acme laptop.
- Lock your screen whenever you leave your device unattended.
- See the IT Security Policy for full requirements.

## 5. Availability

- Core collaboration hours are **10:00–15:00 local time**.
- Keep your calendar and chat status up to date.
