# Acme Corp Code of Conduct

Owner: Legal & Compliance (HR) · Version 6.0

## 1. Our principles

We act with integrity, treat each other with respect, protect our customers' data, and comply with the law everywhere we operate.

## 2. Gifts and hospitality

- You may accept gifts or hospitality worth up to **EUR 50** per occasion from business partners.
- Anything above EUR 50 must be declared in the Gifts Register and approved by Compliance.
- Cash or cash equivalents (gift cards, vouchers) must never be accepted, regardless of value.
- Never offer gifts to public officials without prior Compliance approval.

## 3. Conflicts of interest

- Disclose any personal relationship, side business or investment that could influence your decisions at Acme.
- Secondary employment requires written approval from your manager and HR.

## 4. Respect at work

- Acme has zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination and bullying.
- Report concerns to your manager, HR, or the confidential **Speak Up hotline** (available 24/7, anonymous reporting possible).
- Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is prohibited.

## 5. Confidential information

- Keep Acme and customer information confidential, also after you leave the company.
- Do not discuss non-public financial results outside Acme.

## 6. Consequences

Breaches of this Code may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including dismissal.

Speak Up hotline: speakup.acme.example
