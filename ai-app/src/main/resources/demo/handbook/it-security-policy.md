# Acme Corp IT Security Policy

Owner: Information Security (IT) · Version 5.3 · Mandatory for all staff and contractors

## 1. Passwords and authentication

- Passwords must be at least **14 characters** long; passphrases are encouraged.
- **Multi-factor authentication (MFA)** is mandatory for email, VPN, and all SaaS applications.
- Passwords are **not** rotated on a fixed schedule; change your password immediately if you suspect it was exposed.
- Never share passwords. Use the company password manager (1Password Business).
- Password resets for another employee can only be performed by the IT helpdesk after manager approval.

## 2. Lost or stolen devices

- Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone **within 1 hour** to the IT helpdesk (ext. 4357) or security@acme.example.
- IT will remotely lock and wipe the device. File a police report for stolen devices within 48 hours.

## 3. Phishing and suspicious email

- Use the **"Report Phish" button** in Outlook for any suspicious email; do not forward it.
- Never enter your Acme credentials on a page you reached from an email link.

## 4. Software and data

- Install software only through the **Company Portal**. Admin rights are granted temporarily on request.
- **USB storage devices are blocked** on Acme laptops; use OneDrive or SharePoint to share files.
- Customer data must never be pasted into public AI tools. Use Acme's approved internal AI assistant.
- Data classification: Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted. Restricted data must be encrypted at rest and in transit.

## 5. Network

- Use the Acme VPN on any public or home network that is shared with others.
- Personal devices may access email and chat only after enrolling in Intune (BYOD).

## 6. Laptop lifecycle

- Standard laptops are replaced every **4 years**, or earlier if out of warranty and faulty.
- Faulty laptops under warranty are repaired by the vendor; IT provides a spare loaner laptop while repairs take place.

Report incidents: security@acme.example · IT helpdesk ext. 4357
