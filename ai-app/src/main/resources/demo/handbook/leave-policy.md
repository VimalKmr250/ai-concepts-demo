# Acme Corp Leave Policy

Owner: People Team (HR) · Version 4.2 · Applies to all permanent employees

## 1. Annual leave

- Full-time employees receive **26 working days** of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus local public holidays.
- Part-time employees receive annual leave pro rata to their contracted hours.
- After **5 years of service**, annual leave increases to **28 working days**.
- Leave must be requested in Workday at least **10 working days in advance** for absences longer than 3 days.

## 2. Carry-over of unused leave

- Employees may carry over a maximum of **5 unused days** into the next calendar year.
- Carried-over days must be taken by **31 March** of the following year, otherwise they expire.
- Exceptions (for example, long-term illness that prevented taking leave) require written approval from the People Team.
- Unused leave is not paid out, except on termination of employment where local law requires it.

## 3. Sick leave

- Notify your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence.
- A doctor's certificate is required from the **4th consecutive calendar day** of sickness.
- Acme pays full salary for up to **30 working days** of sick leave per year; after that, statutory sick pay rules apply.

## 4. Parental leave

- The primary caregiver receives **20 weeks of fully paid** parental leave.
- The secondary caregiver receives **6 weeks of fully paid** parental leave, to be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.
- Parental leave can be combined with up to 4 weeks of phased return at 60% hours on full pay.

## 5. Other leave

- **Bereavement leave:** up to 5 paid days for the loss of an immediate family member.
- **Volunteering:** 2 paid days per year for volunteering with a registered charity.
- **Unpaid leave / sabbatical:** after 3 years of service, employees can request up to 3 months of unpaid leave, subject to manager and HR approval.
